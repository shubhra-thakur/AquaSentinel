Utility-Based Decision Support

Decision Explanation

AquaSentinel assigns a priority score using water-loss percentage, pressure readings, flow differences and historical repair evidence.

Higher scores indicate that a zone may need earlier investigation. These scores support human decision-making and do not represent actual water savings.
